# Biological design constraints, not folklore

- Mammalian corpus callosum: bidirectional communication, local excitation and inhibition, adaptive lateralization, and activity-dependent plasticity. The new system implements bounded **analogues**, not a full mammalian brain.
- Corvid/avian pathway: different commissural anatomy is evidence that a human-specific callosal wiring pattern is not strictly required for complex cognition.
- Octopus: peripheral neural circuits and a hierarchy of central controls motivate partial local autonomy, not a claim of hemispheric anatomy identical to humans.
- Timing gates: speculative computational analogue of cross-area synchronization. Phase alignment alone cannot establish truth or semantic equivalence.
- Dynamic pathway learning: use **external** prediction correctness to change routing gain and sparse projection strengths, not agreement between the modules.
